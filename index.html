Our Identity

More Than Just A Club

Tango FC is built on a foundation of discipline, community, and the relentless pursuit of excellence.

Founded in 2015 with a vision to transform Masvingo football, Tango FC has quickly risen to become the MOSSL Champions. Our commitment to excellence — both on and off the pitch — has earned us five league titles, countless unforgettable moments, and the respect of an entire city.

"Success is a journey, not a destination. We play for the badge, the city, and the future."

We believe in developing not just skilled players, but well-rounded individuals who contribute positively to our community. The Tango badge represents more than silverware — it represents character, discipline, and unity.
