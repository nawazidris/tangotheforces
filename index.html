About Tango FC

More than a club — a family united by passion

Founded in 2015, Tango Football Club has grown from humble beginnings to become Masvingo's most successful football organisation. Our commitment to excellence — both on and off the pitch — has earned us five league titles and countless unforgettable moments.

We believe in developing not just skilled players, but well-rounded individuals who contribute positively to our community. The Tango badge represents more than silverware — it represents character, discipline, and unity.
